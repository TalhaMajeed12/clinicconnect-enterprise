# ClinicConnect Business Rules

---

# 1. Authentication

## 1.1 Login

- Every user must log in using their registered email address and password.
- Passwords must never be stored in plain text.
- Passwords must be hashed using Argon2.
- Accounts marked as Inactive cannot log in.
- Accounts marked as Suspended cannot log in.
- Five consecutive failed login attempts lock the account for 15 minutes.
- Every successful login is recorded in the Audit Log.
- Every failed login attempt is recorded.

---

## 1.2 Password Policy

- Minimum 8 characters.
- At least one uppercase letter.
- At least one lowercase letter.
- At least one number.
- At least one special character.

---

## 1.3 Roles

System Administrator
- Full system access.

Administrator
- Manage clinic operations.
- Manage users.
- View reports.

Receptionist
- Register patients.
- Schedule appointments.
- Manage billing.
- Cannot access clinical notes.

Clinician
- Access assigned patients.
- Create consultations.
- Record diagnoses.
- Issue prescriptions.
- Schedule follow-ups.

Patient
- Access only their own records.
- View appointments.
- View prescriptions.
- View invoices.
- Join video consultations when available.

---

## 1.4 Session Management

- Users must authenticate before accessing protected pages.
- Sessions expire after inactivity.
- Users must log out securely.
- Expired sessions require re-authentication.

---

## 1.5 Audit

The following actions must always be logged:

- Login
- Logout
- Failed login
- Password change
- Password reset
- Account creation
- Account update
- Account deletion
---

# 2. Patient Management

## 2.1 Patient Registration

- Every patient must have a unique Patient ID generated by the system.
- CNIC is optional but must be unique if provided.
- Full Name and Date of Birth are mandatory.
- Gender is mandatory.
- Mobile number is mandatory.
- Emergency contact is optional.
- A patient cannot be registered twice using the same Full Name, Date of Birth, and Mobile Number.

---

## 2.2 Patient Record

Each patient has one master record.

The master record stores:

- Personal Information
- Contact Information
- Emergency Contact
- Medical History
- Allergies
- Chronic Conditions

---

## 2.3 Patient Status

A patient can be:

- Active
- Inactive
- Deceased

Inactive patients:

- Cannot book appointments.
- Cannot log into the patient portal.
- Their medical history remains available to authorized staff.

---

## 2.4 Patient Portal

Patients can:

- View their profile.
- View appointment history.
- View prescriptions.
- View invoices and receipts.
- View consultation summaries approved for patient access.
- Update limited personal information (phone number, address, email).
- They cannot modify any clinical records.

---

## 2.5 Privacy

Patients can only access their own information.

Clinicians can only access records required for patient care.

Administrators cannot modify clinical notes or diagnoses.

Every access to a patient's medical record must be recorded in the Audit Log.
---

# 3. Appointment Management

## 3.1 Appointment Creation

- Every appointment must belong to one patient.
- Every appointment must be assigned to one clinician.
- Every appointment must belong to one branch.
- Appointment date and time are mandatory.
- A clinician cannot have overlapping appointments.
- A patient cannot have overlapping active appointments.

---

## 3.2 Appointment Status

Appointments may have one of the following statuses:

- Scheduled
- Confirmed
- Checked-In
- Completed
- Cancelled
- No Show

Status transitions:

Scheduled
→ Confirmed
→ Checked-In
→ Completed

Scheduled
→ Cancelled

Confirmed
→ Cancelled

Confirmed
→ No Show

Completed appointments cannot be modified.

Cancelled appointments cannot create visits.

---

## 3.3 Appointment Rules

- Appointment duration must be configurable.
- Receptionists can create appointments.
- Administrators can create appointments.
- Clinicians cannot modify completed appointments.
- Patients can request appointments through the Patient Portal.
- Appointment confirmation remains under clinic control.

---

## 3.4 Check-In

When a patient arrives:

- Appointment status changes to Checked-In.
- A Visit record is created.
- The patient enters the clinician's queue.

---

## 3.5 Cancellation

Cancelled appointments:

- Must record the cancellation reason.
- Must record who cancelled the appointment.
- Cannot generate a Visit.
- Cannot generate a Consultation.
- Cannot generate an Invoice.

---

## 3.6 Audit

The following actions must be logged:

- Appointment created
- Appointment updated
- Appointment cancelled
- Appointment confirmed
- Patient checked in
- Appointment marked as No Show
---

# 4. Visit Management

## 4.1 Visit Creation

- A Visit can only be created from a Checked-In appointment.
- Every Visit must belong to one patient.
- Every Visit must belong to one appointment.
- Every Visit must belong to one clinician.
- Every Visit must belong to one branch.

---

## 4.2 Visit Status

A Visit may have one of the following statuses:

- Waiting
- In Progress
- Completed
- Cancelled

Status transitions:

Waiting
→ In Progress
→ Completed

Waiting
→ Cancelled

Completed Visits cannot be modified.

---

## 4.3 Consultation

- A Visit can have only one Consultation.
- A Consultation cannot exist without a Visit.
- A Consultation can only begin when the Visit status is In Progress.

---

## 4.4 Queue Management

Patients enter the queue after Check-In.

The queue is ordered by:

1. Appointment Time
2. Check-In Time

Clinicians can call the next patient from the queue.

---

## 4.5 Visit Completion

A Visit is completed only after:

- Consultation is completed.
- Diagnosis is recorded (if applicable).
- Prescription is finalized (if required).
- Follow-up decision is recorded.
- Billing is generated (if applicable).

---

## 4.6 Audit

The following actions must be logged:

- Visit created
- Visit started
- Visit completed
- Visit cancelled
- Patient added to queue
- Patient removed from queue
---

# 5. Consultation

## 5.1 Consultation Creation

- A Consultation can only be created from an active Visit.
- Every Consultation belongs to one Visit.
- Every Consultation belongs to one Patient.
- Every Consultation belongs to one Clinician.
- A Visit can have only one Consultation.

---

## 5.2 Consultation Workflow

During a Consultation, the clinician may:

- Record Vital Signs.
- Record Diagnoses.
- Write Clinical Notes.
- Issue Prescriptions.
- Schedule Follow-ups.

These actions may be performed in any order, but the Consultation cannot be completed until all mandatory information has been recorded.

---

## 5.3 Consultation Completion

Before marking a Consultation as Completed:

- The clinician must record at least one Clinical Note.
- If medication is prescribed, a Prescription must exist.
- If a follow-up is required, a Follow-up record must be created.
- The Consultation must be electronically signed by the responsible clinician.

Completed Consultations cannot be edited.

If corrections are required after completion, an amendment must be recorded instead of modifying the original Consultation.

---

## 5.4 Clinical Responsibility

Only the assigned Clinician may:

- Record Diagnoses.
- Issue Prescriptions.
- Complete the Consultation.

Receptionists and Administrators cannot modify clinical information.

---

## 5.5 Audit

The following actions must be logged:

- Consultation started
- Consultation updated
- Consultation completed
- Diagnosis added
- Prescription issued
- Clinical Note added
- Follow-up scheduled
---

# 6. Electronic Health Record (EHR)

## 6.1 Ownership

The Electronic Health Record belongs to the patient.

ClinicConnect stores and protects the record on behalf of the healthcare organization.

---

## 6.2 Record Integrity

Clinical records must never be physically deleted.

If information requires correction:

- Record an amendment.
- Preserve the original record.
- Record who made the amendment.
- Record when the amendment was made.
- Record the reason for the amendment.

---

## 6.3 Clinical Data

The EHR may contain:

- Vital Signs
- Diagnoses
- Prescriptions
- Clinical Notes
- Follow-ups
- Visit History
- Consultation History

Each record must be linked to its originating Consultation.

---

## 6.4 Access Control

Patients may:

- View approved medical records.
- View prescriptions.
- View follow-up instructions.
- View visit history.

Patients cannot:

- Modify any clinical information.
- Delete records.
- Hide records.

Clinicians may:

- Access records of patients under their care.
- Create new clinical records.
- Amend existing records where permitted.

Receptionists cannot access clinical notes, diagnoses, or prescriptions.

---

## 6.5 Confidentiality

Every access to an EHR must be logged.

The system must record:

- User
- Patient
- Date and time
- Action performed

Unauthorized access attempts must also be recorded.

---

## 6.6 Data Retention

Patient medical records must remain available even if:

- The patient account becomes inactive.
- The patient changes contact information.
- The patient is no longer receiving treatment.

Medical records must never be permanently removed through the application.
---

# 7. Billing

## 7.1 Invoice Generation

- An Invoice can only be generated after a Consultation has been completed.
- Each Consultation can generate only one Invoice.
- Every Invoice must belong to one Patient.
- Every Invoice must belong to one Consultation.

---

## 7.2 Invoice Items

An Invoice may contain one or more charge items, including:

- Consultation Fee
- Procedure Charges
- Medicine Charges
- Service Charges
- Laboratory Charges (Future)
- Other Charges

Each Invoice Item must include:

- Description
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Discount
- Total Price

---

## 7.3 Payments

An Invoice may be paid:

- In full.
- In multiple partial payments.

Accepted payment methods:

- Cash
- Card
- Bank Transfer
- JazzCash
- Easypaisa

Every payment must record:

- Payment Date
- Amount
- Payment Method
- Received By
- Payment Reference

---

## 7.4 Receipts

A Receipt is generated only after a successful payment.

Each Receipt must include:

- Receipt Number
- Payment Reference
- Amount Received
- Receipt Date

Receipts cannot be edited after generation.

If a correction is required, a new Receipt or refund record must be created according to clinic policy.

---

## 7.5 Invoice Status

Invoices may have one of the following statuses:

- Unpaid
- Partially Paid
- Paid
- Cancelled

Status changes must occur automatically based on recorded payments.

---

## 7.6 Refunds

Refunds must:

- Record the refund amount.
- Record the refund reason.
- Record the authorized user.
- Be linked to the original Payment.

Refund history must never be deleted.

---

## 7.7 Audit

The following actions must be logged:

- Invoice created
- Invoice updated
- Payment received
- Receipt generated
- Refund processed
---

# 9. Clinician Portal

## 9.1 Access

Only authenticated clinicians may access the Clinician Portal.

Clinicians may only access patients assigned to them or patients they are authorized to treat.

---

## 9.2 Dashboard

The Clinician Dashboard displays:

- Today's Appointments
- Waiting Patients
- Upcoming Follow-ups
- Recent Consultations
- Pending Clinical Tasks

---

## 9.3 Patient Access

Clinicians may:

- Search patients.
- View complete medical history.
- View previous consultations.
- View diagnoses.
- View prescriptions.
- View laboratory results (Future).
- View billing summary (read-only).

---

## 9.4 Consultation

Clinicians may:

- Start a consultation.
- Record vital signs (if required).
- Add diagnoses.
- Write clinical notes.
- Issue prescriptions.
- Schedule follow-ups.
- Complete consultations.

Only the assigned clinician may complete a consultation.

---

## 9.5 Prescription Management

Clinicians may:

- Create prescriptions.
- Modify prescriptions before the consultation is completed.
- Cancel draft prescriptions.

Issued prescriptions become part of the permanent medical record.

---

## 9.6 Follow-up Management

Clinicians may:

- Schedule clinic follow-ups.
- Schedule video consultations.
- Schedule telephone follow-ups.
- Record follow-up instructions.

---

## 9.7 Telemedicine

Clinicians may:

- Start video consultations.
- Admit patients into the virtual consultation room.
- Share consultation notes during the session.
- End the consultation.

Video consultation records become part of the patient's consultation history.

---

## 9.8 Restrictions

Clinicians cannot:

- Delete patient records.
- Delete consultations.
- Delete diagnoses.
- Delete prescriptions.
- Modify completed consultations.
- Access system administration functions.

---

## 9.9 Audit

The following actions must be logged:

- Patient record viewed
- Consultation started
- Consultation completed
- Diagnosis added
- Prescription issued
- Follow-up scheduled
- Video consultation started
- Video consultation ended
---

# 10. Administrator

## 10.1 Access

Only authenticated Administrators and System Administrators may access the Admin Portal.

Role permissions determine which administrative functions are available.

---

## 10.2 User Management

Administrators may:

- Create user accounts.
- Activate user accounts.
- Deactivate user accounts.
- Reset user passwords.
- Assign roles.
- Update user profiles.

Administrators cannot:

- View user passwords.
- Modify completed clinical records.
- Alter audit logs.

---

## 10.3 Organization Management

Administrators may:

- Manage organization information.
- Manage branch information.
- Configure clinic working hours.
- Configure appointment durations.
- Configure holidays.

---

## 10.4 Clinician Management

Administrators may:

- Add clinicians.
- Update clinician profiles.
- Assign specialties.
- Activate or deactivate clinicians.

Administrators cannot modify clinical decisions made by clinicians.

---

## 10.5 Reporting

Administrators may access:

- Patient Statistics
- Appointment Reports
- Billing Reports
- Revenue Reports
- Clinician Workload Reports
- Audit Reports

Reports are read-only.

---

## 10.6 System Configuration

Administrators may configure:

- Payment methods.
- Notification settings.
- Email settings.
- SMS settings.
- Security policies.
- Password policies.
- Session timeout.

---

## 10.7 Backup & Recovery

System Administrators may:

- Create database backups.
- Restore database backups.
- Schedule automatic backups.

Every backup and restore operation must be logged.

---

## 10.8 Restrictions

Administrators cannot:

- Edit completed consultations.
- Edit diagnoses.
- Edit prescriptions.
- Delete medical records.
- Delete audit logs.

---

## 10.9 Audit

The following actions must be logged:

- User created
- User updated
- User deactivated
- Role changed
- Branch created
- Branch updated
- System setting changed
- Backup created
- Backup restored
